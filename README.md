## 🚀 Overview
Truck-Kun is a thrilling 3D endless runner game where players ride atop a speeding truck through a desert wasteland, dodging deadly street lights and collecting coins in a fast-paced, lane-based survival challenge. Inspired by the chaotic fun of Gang Beasts, Truck-Kun blends intense reflex-based gameplay with dynamic environmental effects like sandstorms and seamless day-night transitions for an immersive and visually compelling experience.

## ❓ Problem Statement
Endless runner games often suffer from repetitive design and lack of immersion. Truck-Kun redefines this genre by introducing visually rich environments, optimized performance, and dynamic mechanics that require both quick reactions and strategic thinking. With procedurally spawning terrain, power-ups, and immersive particle and lighting systems, Truck-Kun creates a fresh and exciting take on the classic runner formula.

## 🎯 Key Objectives
- Deliver a high-performance, immersive 3D endless runner experience using Unity.
- Introduce dynamic environmental elements like sandstorms and time-of-day transitions.
- Create engaging and replayable gameplay through obstacle variety, power-ups, and pickups.
- Maintain optimal performance through object pooling and dynamic spawning systems.

## 🛠 Technologies Used
- **Engine**: Unity
- **Programming**: C#
- **Visual Effects**: Unity Particle System, Shader Graph
- **Platform**: PC (expandable to mobile)

## 🧠 Features & Capabilities
- 🚚 **Gameplay Mechanics**
  - **Lane Navigation**: Players can switch between two lanes to dodge obstacles and reach pickups.
  - **Movement**: Jumping and lateral movement are used to survive and maximize coin collection.
  - **Game Over**: Falling off the truck results in an instant game over, raising the stakes with each move.

- 🌪️ **Dynamic Environment**
  - **Sandstorm Simulation**: Designed using Unity’s Particle System for visual immersion.
  - **Day-Night Cycle**: Smooth real-time transitions implemented via Shader Graph to keep the scenery fresh and engaging.

- 🎯 **Obstacles & Pickups**
  - **Obstacles**: Street lights block your path—dodge or leap to survive.
  - **Coins**: Spawn at random intervals on trucks, rewarding timing and precision.
  - **Shield Power-Up**: Grants temporary invincibility to plow through obstacles safely.
  - **Coin Magnet**: Temporarily attracts all nearby coins, boosting score potential.

## ⚙️ Performance Optimization
- **Dynamic Spawning**: New environment sections spawn ahead of the player while outdated ones are despawned.
- **Object Pooling**: Ensures smooth gameplay even with continuous spawning and destruction of game objects.

## 🌍 Scope of the Project
This solo-developed game project aims to:

- **Enhance Game Design Skills**: Practice and demonstrate proficiency in game mechanics, optimization, and environmental storytelling.
- **Create an Engaging Casual Game**: Build a replayable, visually immersive game that’s easy to pick up but hard to master.
- **Experiment with Unity Features**: Leverage Unity’s powerful visual and performance tools—Particle Systems, Shader Graph, and real-time optimization.
